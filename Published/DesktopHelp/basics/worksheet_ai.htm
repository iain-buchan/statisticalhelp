    Worksheet Learn and AI Assist

    The worksheet toolbar has two ways to get guidance. Learn opens the lesson library, including worked examples and practice questions that you can use without an AI connection. AI Assist opens a conversation for the current workbook. While it is open, AI Assist follows the workbook you bring to the front after any reply finishes, restoring that workbook’s conversation and sharing choice.

    Ask a question

    On Mac, leave Share workbook data unticked to send only the current question and get guidance from the packaged Method Selection guide. Include the full question each time, with any details AI Assist asked for. To include data, check the named workbook for person identifiers and confirm sharing. If two open workbooks have the same name, AI Assist shows their folders. A workbook keeps its own conversation, draft and confirmation when you switch away and return. An active reply stays with the workbook it started in. Choose Stop to end it and follow the most recently selected workbook. Closing the pane also stops the reply.

    On Windows, an empty workbook offers Open example workbook and Workbook help. When the workbook has data, choose Help me choose a method to start with Statistical Method Selection, or Browse methods to search the available commands. Below Ask a question or Ask a question about your data, the selection label follows your worksheet, for example Selection: Sheet!A1:B6 or Current cell: Sheet!A1.

    On Windows, ask how to organise or analyse your data. Leave Share open workbook data unticked to ask a question without sharing the workbook. When you press Send, ChatGPT receives your question and can use the relevant Help. It can also draw on general statistical and R knowledge. Leave names, patient identifiers and other confidential information out of your question. In ordinary use, the recent conversation also provides context. Choose New conversation to leave earlier shared content out.

    If you ask the assistant to choose a test, it may first ask what you measured, what you want to compare or how the observations are related. Explain the meaning of any numeric codes, your comparison goal and which measurements come from the same people or units. On Windows, if the question offers answers such as Compare means, Numeric measurement or Matched observations, select the answer that describes your study. Use I’m not sure when you need help deciding. If you already know which method you need, ask for it by name.

    On Windows, if you want advice based on worksheet values, tick Share open workbook data above the question box. The workbook name appears in parentheses. Before sharing that workbook for the first time during a session, check it for person identifiers and tick I have checked the documents selected above contain no person identifiers. Remember this confirmation for those documents during this session. This confirms sharing for the named workbook. Each workbook needs its own confirmation, which is remembered when you switch between workbooks. Turn sharing off to stop further workbook reads.

    On Windows, Reply in progress… appears beside Stop while a reply is being prepared or written. Expand Data used under a reply to see the workbook, worksheet and ranges read. (headings) marks first-row text sent as column titles. (part of the worksheet’s used range) marks a partial read. A reply with no worksheet reads shows General guidance · No worksheet cells read for this reply. If a reply stops or fails after reading cells, its note remains, even if the reply shows only [Reply stopped before any text.] or [No reply was received.].

    Open suggested R code

    AI Assist answers questions and leaves worksheet cells unchanged. If a reply includes code, expand the suggested code to see it. Choose Open in R to open an editable script. StatsDirect includes a fixed snapshot of the worksheet values read for that reply. Ask a follow-up to get a new analysis using current approved values.

    Review the code, then choose Run script in the R window. When R finishes, choose Add output to report to save the text output and charts in a StatsDirect report.

    Choose and confirm a method

    Select the cells in the worksheet, then select Choose a method on Mac or Browse methods on Windows. This opens the same list as Analyse on the worksheet toolbar. If a report is active, the picker returns to the workbook associated with the chat and the selected report remains the destination for results. You can search for a method such as Mann–Whitney and select it. AI Assist can also suggest a method from this list in response to a question.

    On Windows, when asked about your study design, select Same people or units if both measurements come from each person or unit, or Separate groups for independent samples. The next card helps you choose a method using the size and distribution of the samples. Select two adjacent visible columns in one worksheet range.

    • For paired data, use Check selected pairs if prompted. The local check counts complete pairs, can show Shapiro–Wilk W and P for up to 100 pairs, and may ask you to review the differences. Above 100 pairs it reports the number outside the 3 × IQR fences, skewness and excess kurtosis. Large sample (more than 100 complete pairs) and Small or medium · Approximately normal differences lead to the paired t test. Non-normal differences leads to Wilcoxon signed ranks and is also available above 100 pairs. Consider Wilcoxon for a shift in location when the differences are roughly symmetric. Use the paired t test for a question about the mean difference.
    • For independent samples, choose Check two sample columns or Use these as separate samples as shown. The card shows each group’s count and can show Shapiro–Wilk for a group with up to 100 observations. Large samples (more than 100 in each group) leads to the two-sample z test, Small or medium · Approximately normal within groups to the unpaired t test, and Non-normal distributions within groups to Mann–Whitney.

    Check the reason under Suggested branch, or follow Review the differences before deciding. Use Change design to go back. The checks run on your computer. If a choice conflicts with the counted sample size, the card explains which choices apply. If the card refreshes after a change to the selection, choose again on the updated card.

    The confirmation in the chat shows the method, its menu location, the worksheet and selected cells, and the destination when the method produces a report. Check these details, then choose Continue on Mac or Continue to analysis on Windows. StatsDirect opens the usual controls or runs the method with the available settings. It asks you to choose the method again if the worksheet, selection or report destination has changed.

    On Windows, use Change sample choice in a confirmation to return to the sample card, or Change design when that option appears.

    AI Assist can read approved worksheet data. On Windows, a native Data command may change worksheet cells after you confirm and use its usual StatsDirect controls. Choosing a method and continuing in StatsDirect sends no workbook data or results to ChatGPT.

    Using AI Assist under an organisation's policy

    On Mac, an administrator can turn off online AI. The lesson library, local Help and the Analysis menu stay available.

    On Windows, when your organisation restricts data sharing (level 2), AI Assist can answer questions using packaged Help. You can still use the local method cards, check a selection and confirm and run an analysis. Workbook sharing is unavailable. Each request sends the latest question and relevant Help. Your workbook, earlier conversation and local results stay on your computer. If the assistant asks for more information, send the full question again in one message with all the requested non-confidential details, such as what you measured, what you want to compare and how the observations are related.

    On Windows, when your organisation restricts AI (level 3), AI Assist cannot connect. Learn, local help, and the analyses on the Analysis menu remain available. See Script AI for sign-in and Windows policy details.
