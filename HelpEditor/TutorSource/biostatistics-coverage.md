# Biostatistics and epidemiology coverage

Source check dated 15 November 2026. This guide maps public examination documents to the statistics, epidemiology and research interpretation taught in StatsDirect. The activities and questions use original wording and fictional data. Exam bodies own their published examples. Link to them from the tutor and use them to check format and scope.

## United Kingdom

- **Medical undergraduate and Medical Licensing Assessment (MLA).** For exams from September 2026, the GMC puts interpretation and use of research, data and statistics within social and population health. *Outcomes for graduates*, sections 25 and 26, specifies basic epidemiological measurements, prevention and screening, evidence hierarchy, interpretation of common statistical tests, critical appraisal, study design, communication and stratified risk. The Medical Schools Council’s Applied Knowledge Test (AKT) has 200 five-option single-best-answer items in two papers. The published sampling grid gives minimum item counts by area of clinical practice and provides no statistics-only quota. The 2026 practice papers show the style. [GMC content map](https://www.gmc-uk.org/education/medical-licensing-assessment/mla-content-map) · [GMC graduate outcomes](https://www.gmc-uk.org/-/media/documents/dc11326-outcomes-for-graduates-2018_pdf-75040796.pdf/) · [MSC sampling grid](https://www.medschools.ac.uk/wp-content/uploads/2025/05/ms-akt-sampling-grid-updated-2023.pdf) · [MSC practice papers](https://www.medschools.ac.uk/for-students/medical-licensing-assessment/practice-exam-for-the-ms-akt/)
- **MRCP(UK) Part 1.** The Federation indicates approximately five questions out of 200 in the combined statistics, epidemiology and evidence-based medicine category. The five-option written format tests understanding and problem solving. The public blueprint gives the category and indicative allocation. Official Part 1 practice access asks for an email address. The current Part 2 Written blueprint lists clinical specialties without a separate statistics allocation. [Part 1 format and blueprint](https://www.thefederation.uk/examinations/part-1/format) · [Official Part 1 sample access](https://www.thefederation.uk/examinations/part-1/part-1-sample-questions) · [Part 2 Written format and blueprint](https://www.thefederation.uk/examinations/part-2/format)
- **MRCGP Applied Knowledge Test.** From October 2025 the exam has 160 items. Evidence-based practice accounts for 10% and includes interpretation of research data and critical appraisal. The College’s data interpretation topic guide and sample questions show how evidence is presented in primary care. The number in the older data interpretation guide reflects the former 200-item exam. [Current AKT specification](https://www.rcgp.org.uk/mrcgp-exams/applied-knowledge-test/akt-introduction) · [Data interpretation topics](https://www.rcgp.org.uk/getmedia/dba5948c-3b9d-4936-918e-ca8cea1122b4/Data-Interpretation-topics-AKT.pdf) · [Official practice questions](https://www.rcgp.org.uk/mrcgp-exams/applied-knowledge-test/akt-preparing)
- **Faculty of Public Health Diplomate Examination (DFPH, formerly Part A).** The quantitative syllabus explicitly names epidemiology, standardisation, clustered design, screening and diagnostic accuracy, probability and distributions, descriptive statistics, uncertainty and testing, power, correlation, regression, survival and synthesis. Papers I and II assess knowledge and applied skills including critical appraisal and written interpretation. Paper IIB can include MCQs alongside numerical, graphical and short written answers. The 2025–26 specimens include released material from recent sittings. Practise calculations alongside interpreting tables and writing for a specified audience. [DFPH knowledge syllabus](https://www.fph.org.uk/media/3824/oct-2016-diplomate-examination-syllabus_reviewed-2022.pdf) · [Exam structure](https://www.fph.org.uk/training-careers/the-diplomate-dfph-and-final-membership-examination-mfph/the-diplomate-examination-dfph/structure-of-the-diplomate-examination-dfph/) · [Official specimens and mark schemes](https://www.fph.org.uk/training-careers/the-diplomate-dfph-and-final-membership-examination-mfph/the-diplomate-examination-dfph/the-diplomate-examination-dfph-preparation/)
- **Faculty of Public Health Final Membership (MFPH, formerly Part B).** The Objective Structured Public Health Examination (OSPHE) uses six role-play stations. Each has eight minutes of preparation and eight minutes of assessment. Data interpretation, explanation, use and quality are among the topics. Stations on hospital activity and vision screening illustrate audience-specific communication. Use the tutor conversation to practise summarising uncertainty, responding to a challenge and discussing the next action with a particular audience. [OSPHE format](https://www.fph.org.uk/training-careers/the-diplomate-dfph-and-final-membership-examination-mfph/the-faculty-of-public-health-final-membership-examination/prepare/) · [Official station scenarios](https://www.fph.org.uk/training-careers/the-diplomate-dfph-and-final-membership-examination-mfph/the-faculty-of-public-health-final-membership-examination/practice/)

### Selected UK specialties and recruitment

- **MRCPsych Paper B.** The 2026 format allocates 50 of 150 marks to Critical Review. From January 2027 the broader research methods, statistics, critical review and evidence-based practice domain accounts for 30–36%. The new syllabus explicitly includes survival and multiple comparisons. The College’s Paper B sample questions require login. [Candidate guidance](https://www.rcpsych.ac.uk/training/exams/preparing-for-exams) · [January 2027 blueprint](https://www.rcpsych.ac.uk/docs/default-source/training/examinations/mrcpsych-syllabus-2027/mrcpsych-paper-b-syllabus-2027.pdf)
- **MRCOG.** Knowledge Areas 2 and 5 identify trials, screening, estimation, power and interpretation of maternal, neonatal and perinatal measures. Published sample sets illustrate the written formats. The syllabus does not give a separate statistics weighting. [Syllabus](https://www.rcog.org.uk/media/yeqbkfrw/mrcog-syllabus-and-knowledge-requirements-v10.pdf) · [Part 1 sample SBAs](https://www.rcog.org.uk/careers-and-training/exams/mrcog-our-specialty-training-exam/mrcog-part-1/single-best-answer-sbas/)
- **MRCS Part A.** The guide lists three indicative data interpretation and audit questions in the 180-question Applied Basic Science paper. The Core Surgical Training syllabus names screening, hypothesis testing and common parametric and nonparametric tests. The training syllabus is broader than the individual exam blueprint. [Candidate guide](https://www.intercollegiatemrcsexams.org.uk/about-the-mrcs/candidate-guidance/) · [Core Surgical Training syllabus](https://www.iscp.ac.uk/media/1424/appendix-2-cst-aug-2025-syllabus.pdf)

- **MRCPCH paediatrics.** The Theory and Science examination identifies statistical testing, test choice, study methods and screening. The Applied Knowledge examination includes use and appraisal of clinical evidence. The Foundation of Practice syllabus refers to guidelines. Use paediatric clinical examples to practise those skills. [RCPCH theory syllabi](https://www.rcpch.ac.uk/sites/default/files/2026-02/MRCPCH-theory-exam-syllabi-2023-v3.pdf#page=60)
- **FRCA anaesthesia.** Stage 1 Research and Managing Data names intervals and P values, test choice and power, diagnostic accuracy and ROC curves, and correlation and regression. Stage 2 asks candidates to distinguish clinical from statistical importance and appraise trials, systematic reviews, bias and sample size. [Primary examination syllabus](https://www.rcoa.ac.uk/documents/examination-syllabus-stage-1/research-managing-data-rd) · [Final examination syllabus](https://www.rcoa.ac.uk/documents/examination-syllabus-stage-2/research-managing-data-rmd)
- **MRCEM and FRCEM emergency medicine.** The MRCEM Primary gives 9 of 180 items to the combined evidence-based medicine category, including research methods, critical appraisal and statistics. The intermediate SBA blueprint includes evidence and guidelines. The final FRCEM SBA indicates 10 of 180 items for its broad research learning outcome, including diagnostic studies, trials and synthesis. The published final OSCE example illustrates journal appraisal. [MRCEM Primary regulations](https://rcem.ac.uk/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/MRCEM_Primary_Regulations_and_Information_Pack.pdf#page=8) · [MRCEM SBA regulations](https://rcem.ac.uk/wp-content/uploads/2023/11/MRCEM_SBA_Regulations_and_Information_Pack.pdf#page=8) · [FRCEM final regulations](https://rcem.ac.uk/wp-content/uploads/2023/11/FRCEM_Regulations_and_Information_Pack.pdf#page=11) · [FRCEM OSCE sample](https://rcem.ac.uk/wp-content/uploads/2022/08/FRCEM_OSCE_Sample_Journal_Article_and_Questions.pdf)
- **FFICM intensive care medicine.** The quantitative appendix names description, probability, intervals, test selection, errors, power, study design and meta-analysis. The Faculty has announced a new FCICM exam format starting in July 2027. Use the syllabus that applies to the candidate's sitting. [FFICM syllabus](https://www.ficm.ac.uk/sites/ficm/files/documents/2022-03/FFICM%20Exam%20Syllabus%20v1.0%20March%202022.pdf) · [FCICM transition](https://www.ficm.ac.uk/new-college-exams-approved-by-the-gmc)
- **Specialty Certificate Examinations (SCEs).** The Federation publishes separate blueprints. In Respiratory Medicine, statistics appears within a 20-item combined *Other* category. Medical Oncology allocates eight to a combined research, ethics and economics category. The oncology training curriculum names trial methodology and medical statistics. Use the relevant specialty documents for any weighting. [Respiratory Medicine blueprint](https://www.thefederation.uk/sites/default/files/uploads/Respiratory%20Medicine2018.pdf) · [Medical Oncology blueprint](https://www.thefederation.uk/sites/default/files/uploads/sce-medical-oncology-blueprint_0.pdf) · [Oncology training curriculum](https://www.gmc-uk.org/-/media/documents/medical-oncology-2021-curriculum-final_pdf-86966310.pdf#page=27)
- **Multi-Specialty Recruitment Assessment (MSRA).** The published Clinical Problem Solving component describes Foundation-level clinical decisions across 12 subject areas. Its public description gives no separate statistics allocation. [MSRA Clinical Problem Solving](https://medical.hee.nhs.uk/medical-training-recruitment/medical-specialty-training/multi-specialty-recruitment-assessment-msra/whats-in-the-msra/clinical-problem-solving-paper)

## United States

- **USMLE licensure.** The 2026 common content outline groups biostatistics, epidemiology and population health with interpretation of medical literature. It names study designs, trial types, systematic reviews and meta-analysis, diagnostic and screening test performance, measures of risk, distributions, statistical inference, study interpretation and research ethics. The individual content specifications indicate 4–6% for Step 1’s combined biostatistics and epidemiology/population health category, 3–5% for Step 2 CK’s combined category and 11–13% for Step 3’s combined category. Step 2 CK and Step 3 name interpretation of medical literature in their combined categories. The ranges belong to separate exams. Official sample formats include studies presented as abstracts or pharmaceutical advertisements. [Common outline](https://www.usmle.org/sites/default/files/2022-01/USMLE_Content_Outline_0.pdf#page=37) · [Step 1 specifications](https://www.usmle.org/exam-resources/step-1-materials/step-1-content-outline-and-specifications) · [Step 2 CK specifications](https://www.usmle.org/exam-resources/step-2-ck-materials/step-2-ck-content-outline-specifications) · [Step 3 specifications](https://www.usmle.org/exam-resources/step-3-materials/step-3-exam-content) · [Step 2 CK formats](https://www.usmle.org/exam-resources/step-2-ck-materials/step-2-ck-test-question-formats)
- **American Board of Preventive Medicine (ABPM).** Public Health and General Preventive Medicine lists a 20% combined epidemiology domain, comprising 10% epidemiology, 5% biostatistics and 5% informatics. ABPM also describes 50 common core questions within each 200-question specialty exam. Its core outline and specialty outline are both relevant. [Specialty outline](https://www.theabpm.org/become-certified/exam-content/public-health-and-general-preventive-medicine-content-outline/) · [Core outline](https://www.theabpm.org/become-certified/exam-content/preventive-medicine-core-content-outline/) · [Core exam FAQ](https://www.theabpm.org/certification-exam-faqs/core-examination-faqs/)
- **American Board of Family Medicine (ABFM).** The current blueprint assigns a 5% target to the broad Foundations of Care category, which includes statistics alongside health policy, legal issues and health equity. Preventive Care has a separate 15% target. [ABFM blueprint](https://www.theabfm.org/family-medicine-exam-blueprint/)
- **American Board of Internal Medicine (ABIM).** The internal medicine initial certification blueprint says clinical epidemiology may occur across content areas. Its miscellaneous grouping includes a primarily epidemiology subtopic listed below 2%. The Fall 2026 expanded critical care initial certification blueprint assigns 1% to the broad research, administration and ethics content area, which includes statistical analysis. It separately lists 5% for the clinical task category combining risk assessment, prognosis and epidemiology. These are different dimensions of the blueprint. [Internal Medicine blueprint](https://www.abim.org/Media/h5whkrfe/internal-medicine.pdf) · [Critical Care Medicine expanded blueprint](https://www.abim.org/media/smqffa55/critical-care-medicine-cert-expanded.pdf) · [Implementation timetable](https://www.abim.org/about/abim-exams/blueprints/expanded-blueprints/)
- **American Board of Anesthesiology (ABA).** The written initial certification outline includes measurement and descriptive statistics, error types and power, test selection, adjustment and regression. These support teaching the handling of continuous, categorical and repeated perioperative outcomes. [ABA initial certification outline](https://www.theaba.org/wp-content/uploads/2023/01/Initial_Certification_Content_Outline-4.pdf)
- **American Board of Emergency Medicine (ABEM).** The 2022 practice model lists evidence-based medicine, interpretation of medical literature, research and quality improvement among other competencies. The Qualifying Exam allocates 2% to the broader Other Components category, with no separate figure for these skills. Acute diagnosis, testing and communicating risk supply practice contexts. [ABEM practice model, section 20.2](https://www.abem.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/07/2022-EM-Model-for-Website.pdf#page=51) · [ABEM Qualifying Exam](https://www.abem.org/get-certified/qualifying-exam/)
- **American Board of Pediatrics (ABP).** The board’s public outline returned HTTP 403 during this review. The ACGME Pediatrics Milestones describe training expectations for forming clinical questions and appraising and applying evidence. Use those milestones as training context and leave exam weighting unspecified. [ABP general pediatrics outline](https://www.abp.org/content/general-pediatrics-content-outline) · [ACGME Pediatrics Milestones](https://www.acgme.org/Specialties/Pediatrics/Milestones)

- **American Board of Psychiatry and Neurology (ABPN).** The initial Psychiatry certification specification includes biostatistics and research design within a broad 4–6% behavioural and social sciences category. Epidemiology also appears in the clinical aspects of disorders. The board publishes a separate continuing certification specification. [Initial Psychiatry specification](https://abpn.org/wp-content/uploads/Psychiatry-Certification-Content-Specifications.pdf) · [Continuing Psychiatry assessment](https://abpn.org/maintain-certification/taking-a-cc-exam/specialty-cc-exams/maintenance-of-certification-in-psychiatry/)
- **American Board of Surgery (ABS).** The General Surgery Qualifying Examination includes biostatistics and evaluation of evidence within the broad 5% Miscellaneous Topics category. The subsequent Certifying Exam evaluates clinical reasoning. The board publishes a separate continuing certification programme. [Qualifying Examination outline](https://www.absurgery.org/wp-content/uploads/2023/01/GS-QE.pdf#page=2) · [Certifying Exam](https://www.absurgery.org/get-certified/general-surgery/certifying-exam/) · [Continuous Certification assessments](https://www.absurgery.org/stay-certified/continuous-certification-assessments/)
- **American Board of Obstetrics and Gynecology (ABOG).** The 2026 Specialty Qualifying Examination includes interpretation and use of data as a skill. Its published weighting is roughly one third each for obstetrics, gynecology and office practice. Continuing Certification separately includes appraisal of peer-reviewed articles. Learners should follow the board's terms for live assessments, including the restriction on third-party assistance. [2026 Qualifying bulletin](https://www.abog.org/docs/default-source/bulletins/2026/specialty/2026-specialty-qe-bulletin9661dabd-d623-42cd-945a-e26ed04c6d8a.pdf?sfvrsn=1d960505_1) · [Continuing Certification article assessments](https://www.abog.org/continuing-certification/cc-four-parts/lifelong-learning-self-assessment) · [Continuing Certification terms](https://www.abog.org/resources/policies/terms-of-application-for-continuing-certification)

Specialty certification and licensure each have their own documents. The American Board of Medical Specialties directory maps specialties to boards. Check the issuing board, examination stage, effective date and whether a figure describes a domain or a task before adding it to the tutor. [ABMS specialty and subspecialty directory](https://abms.org/member-boards/specialty-subspecialty-certificates/)

## Lesson map

| Skill | Lesson IDs in the shared tutor |
| --- | --- |
| Classify data, choose a display, describe centre and variation, recognise probability models and sampling limitations | `describe-data`, `precision` |
| Select a method using outcome, estimand and design. Recognise paired, independent and clustered comparisons, sparse counts and correlation assumptions. | `choose-analysis`, `paired`, `dependent-samples`, `regression` |
| Interpret P values, intervals, power, type I and II errors and practical effect size | `precision`, `risk`, `trial-power`, `uncertainty-sensitivity` |
| Distinguish observational, interventional and surveillance designs. Explain selection, confounding, mediators and colliders. | `study-designs`, `epidemiology`, `causal`, `causal-adjustment`, `regression`, `appraisal`, `missing` |
| Read a trial report, identify its population, denominators and outcomes, and explain benefits and harms | `trial-appraisal`, `clinical-abstract`, `trial-power`, `risk`, `appraisal` |
| Use 2×2 diagnostic data, threshold trade-offs, likelihood ratios and prior risk. Appraise a screening programme and its effects. | `diagnostic`, `diagnostic-evidence`, `screening-programmes`, `population-impact` |
| Compare risks, rates, reference populations and age-standardised estimates. Interpret surveillance and outbreak evidence. | `epidemiology`, `rates`, `surveillance-outbreaks`, `population-impact` |
| Recognise repeated measures and cluster designs, estimate design effects and use survey weights appropriately | `dependent-samples`, `paired`, `precision` |
| Read linear, logistic and count models, interpret adjusted results and evaluate prediction | `regression`, `regression-models`, `validation`, `causal-adjustment` |
| Interpret censored follow-up, hazard ratios and competing events | `survival-competing-risks` |
| Assess agreement between measurement methods or raters. Interpret audit, service indicators and process charts. | `paired`, `measurement-agreement`, `audit-spc` |
| Read systematic reviews and forest plots, interpret heterogeneity and consider selective reporting | `meta`, `evidence-synthesis` |
| Choose quantitative, qualitative or mixed methods and recognise safeguards for reproducible and ethical research | `study-designs`, `research-integrity` |
| Explain results in plain language and relate uncertainty to a patient or population decision | `clinical-abstract`, `risk`, `screening-programmes`, `surveillance-outbreaks`, `population-impact`. Use the public-health conversation exercise for role-play. |

Many official outlines also cover territory requiring separate study. Among the advanced areas this map introduces only selectively are full complex-survey analysis, time series and spatial epidemiology, GIS and informatics, costing and health-economic models, regulatory requirements and specialty-specific patient management. Use the coverage map for biostatistics practice alongside the examining body's current documents and study of the wider clinical specialty. The tutor uses public sources and original items only. It has no access to confidential exam banks. The results and conversational feedback are formative.

## Reviewing and publishing course changes

1. Open [the expanded working course](biostatistics-course.json) for review and select that file explicitly for the next shared publication. It contains the baseline 12 lessons and 35 questions plus 19 new lessons and 57 new questions. Keep the immutable seed, the previous working starter, stable question and lesson IDs, learning pathways and source provenance intact. For new examples, record the official scope source separately from original data, explanation and feedback.
2. Review the exact version of each changed item in the Help Editor. Check the key and all distractors, identify assumptions and the estimand, check arithmetic independently, and run each R exercise with `Rscript --vanilla` in a temporary working directory. Record the actual checks and the reviewer. When making an interval comparison with StatsDirect, verify the method as well as the estimate. Keep external subject expertise and formal accreditation claims tied to actual review.
3. Use the Help Builder's `validate-course --course <source.json>` for runtime validation. Preparing or publishing a shared content revision uses the Help Editor's content publishing workflow or the CLI `publish-content`. Review a prepared publication with `--prepare-only` before delivery. The builder generates `Published/Tutor` and its manifest from the reviewed source. Follow the [tutor authoring README](README.md) and [Help Editor README](../README.md) for the applicable publication route.
4. Each app consumes an explicit StatisticalHelp revision. After a shared publication is ready, verify the source and content pins and run the shared Learning tests from the normal Mac and Windows hosts. The [shared Learning README](../../Learning/README.md) describes the host contract and test entry points. Test the learner route from selecting a lesson to submitting a question and opening its StatsDirect example or R script.
