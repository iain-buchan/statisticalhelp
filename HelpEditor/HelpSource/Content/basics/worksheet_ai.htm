Worksheet Learn and AI Assist

The Windows worksheet toolbar has two ways to get guidance. Learn opens the lesson library, including worked examples and practice questions that you can use without an AI connection. AI Assist opens a conversation for the current workbook.

Ask a question

Ask how to organise or analyse your data. At the start of a conversation the assistant uses a short guide based on Statistical Method Selection to help you choose an analysis. It can search and read the entire bundled Help and draw on general statistical and R knowledge for questions beyond StatsDirect. Leave Share workbook data with AI unticked to ask a question without sharing the workbook. When you press Send, ChatGPT receives your question and can use the relevant Help. Leave names, patient identifiers and other confidential information out of your question. In ordinary use, the recent conversation also provides context. Choose New conversation to leave earlier shared content out.

If you ask the assistant to choose a test, it may first ask what you measured and how it was recorded (for example a quantitative measurement, an ordered rating or a category), what you want to compare, or how the observations are related (for example independent groups or matched pairs). Explain the meaning of any numeric codes, your comparison goal, and which measurements come from the same people or units. If you already know which method you need, ask for it by name.

If you want advice based on worksheet values, tick Share workbook data with AI above the question box. When you enable sharing for a workbook for the first time in the session, the confirmation dialog names that workbook and asks you to confirm that you have removed person identifiers. This confirmation covers the named workbook for the current application session. Another open workbook needs its own confirmation. Simply visiting it does not remove the first workbook's confirmation. Turn sharing off to stop further workbook reads.

Open suggested R code

AI Assist answers questions and leaves worksheet cells unchanged. If a reply includes code, expand Show R code to see it. Choose Open in R to open an editable script. StatsDirect includes a fixed snapshot of the worksheet values read for that reply. Ask a follow-up to get a new analysis using current approved values.

Review the code, then choose Run script in the R window. When R finishes, choose Add output to report to save the text output and charts in a StatsDirect report.

Choose and confirm a method

Select the cells in the worksheet, then choose Choose a method in the chat. This opens the same list as Analyse on the worksheet toolbar. If a report is active, Choose a method returns to the workbook associated with the chat. The selected report remains the destination for results. You can search for a method such as Mann–Whitney and select it. AI Assist can also suggest a method from this list in response to a question.

To compare means in two samples, choose Compare two samples at the start of a new conversation. Choose Same people for a paired t test or Separate groups for an unpaired t test. Use Change design if you need to change that choice.

The confirmation in the chat shows the method, its menu location, the worksheet and selected cells, and the destination when the method produces a report. Check these details, then choose Continue to analysis. StatsDirect opens the usual controls or runs the method with the available settings. It asks you to choose the method again if the worksheet, selection or report destination has changed.

AI Assist can read approved worksheet data. A native Data command may change worksheet cells after you confirm and use its usual StatsDirect controls. Choosing a method and continuing in StatsDirect sends no workbook data or results to ChatGPT.

Using AI Assist under an organisation's policy

When your organisation restricts data sharing (level 2), AI Assist can still answer questions using packaged Help and suggest an analysis for you to review and run locally. Workbook sharing is unavailable. Each request sends the question you type and relevant Help, while your workbook, earlier conversation and local results stay on your computer.

When your organisation restricts AI (level 3), AI Assist cannot connect. Learn, local help, and the analyses on the Analysis menu remain available. See Script AI for sign-in and Windows policy details.
