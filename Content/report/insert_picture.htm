Insert picture

 

To insert a clipboard picture, put the cursor in an editable report section, or choose Add text to create one, then choose Paste or press Ctrl+V. SVG is kept as vector artwork; PNG and bitmap pictures are embedded as PNG. For a chart in R output, right-click its picture and choose Copy image first. The saved report contains its own copy. In StatsDirect 5.2.3, select the picture and press Enter, or put the cursor immediately after it and press Enter, to continue writing in a normal paragraph below it in the same result. You do not need Add text for this. Paste and the new paragraph support Undo and Redo. Charts from StatsDirect analyses continue to appear automatically in the report. See the Report window topic.
