Saving a chart

 

Right-click a chart in a report and choose Save chart as SVG or Save chart as PNG. The SVG file is the chart itself, a vector drawing that scales without loss and that browsers and drawing programs open and edit; the PNG is drawn from it at twice its size on the page, for programs that take only pictures. Copy puts a selected chart on the clipboard for pasting into Word or another program.

 

The earlier editor's metafile and bitmap export and its Replay Operation are gone. To take an analysis further, use Continue in R on its result, or run the analysis again from the menu. See the Report window topic for charts in a report.
